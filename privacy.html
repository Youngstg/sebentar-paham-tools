Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Last Updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sebentar Paham Tools ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect your TikTok account via our content management tool.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with TikTok through OAuth 2.0, we may access the following authorized information based on approved scopes:

  • Basic Account Information (user.info.basic): Your TikTok OpenID, display name, and avatar URL to verify account identity.
  • Video Upload & Publishing Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Authorization tokens required to push video files to your TikTok draft or scheduled posts upon your explicit command.
  • OAuth Tokens: Temporary access_token and refresh_token strings stored locally on your machine to maintain API communication.

What We DO NOT Collect: We never collect, store, or have access to your TikTok password, banking/payment information, private direct messages, or contacts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Authenticating your session and verifying that uploads target your designated TikTok account.
  • Transmitting selected educational MP4 video files, captions, and hashtag metadata to the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Scheduling video release times as specified in your content calendar.

No Data Sale or Commercial Profiling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your TikTok account data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your OAuth access credentials and refresh tokens are stored securely in local environment configuration files on your designated deployment. No user data is transferred to external proprietary databases.

4. Third-Party Services: TikTok

Our service uses the official TikTok for Developers API. By connecting your account, you also subject your data processing to the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights: Data Deletion & Revoking Access

You retain full control over your TikTok data at all times. You can revoke our application's access immediately by:

  1. Opening the TikTok App on your mobile device.
  2. Navigating to Profile > Settings and Privacy > Security > Manage App Permissions.
  3. Locating Sebentar Paham Tools (or your custom Developer App name) and selecting Remove Access.

Upon access removal, our stored OAuth tokens become permanently invalid and can no longer interact with your account.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

7. Contact Information
